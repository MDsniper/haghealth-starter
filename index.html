Three Disciplines

One practice. Three lines of care.

Each with its own clinician, its own body of training, and its own treatment plan written around you. No referral required to be seen.

Chiropractic Care

Active, full-body healthcare without drugs or surgery. Manual adjustments and soft-tissue work decrease muscle tension, reduce nerve compression, and restore joint mobility — helping the body return to its natural balance.

Dr. Brenna Bacon Ranieli, DC

Physical Therapy

Direct, one-on-one care for 45 minutes — not a rotation through a gym floor. Examination and individualized plans to promote movement, reduce pain, restore function, and prevent disability before it starts.

Dr. Matthew Ranieli, DPT, MBA

Your Providers

Two doctors. Two North Hagerstown diplomas. One-on-one care.

For the providers and staff, ties to this area span decades to generations — a commitment you can feel the moment you walk in.

Practicing since 2010

Dr. Brenna Bacon Ranieli

Doctor of Chiropractic • Certified Women's Health Specialist

Born and raised in Hagerstown, Dr. Ranieli graduated from North Hagerstown High School in 2004. She attended the University of Maryland, College Park and graduated from the Honors Program in 2007. Pursuing higher education at National University of Health Sciences in Lombard, IL, she received a Doctor of Chiropractic degree in 2010 — the same year she married her high school sweetheart, Matthew. Just a few months later she started her practice and has been providing quality care ever since.

As her family has grown, so has her commitment to our community. She has continued her education and completed extensive coursework in functional nutrition, and has recently completed all requirements to become qualified as a Certified Women's Health Specialist (CWHS). She served as Official Chiropractor of the Hagerstown Suns, 2012–2020, and currently serves her second term on the Antietam Chapter of the DAR.

Best PT, 2021–2025

Dr. Matthew Ranieli

DPT, MBA • Doctor of Physical Therapy

Dr. Matthew Ranieli was voted the Herald-Mail Tri-State's Best Physical Therapist in 2021–2025. He is a former Certified Strength and Conditioning Specialist and is currently certified in neuromuscular taping, instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilization, cupping, the Functional Movement Screen, and Selective Functional Movement Assessment.

Dr. Ranieli has also completed extensive continuing education in manual and manipulation therapy, allowing him to address most musculoskeletal conditions from head to toe, plus a vestibular and dizziness treatment certification. He earned his Doctorate of Physical Therapy from Midwestern University (2012) and his MBA from Western Governors University (2019), and is currently serving his second term as president of the Hagerstown Rotary Club.

Kelly Sauser

Office Manager & Billing Specialist

Kelly has been an office manager for over 12 years in the chiropractic field, and worked with two local chiropractors while obtaining her Maryland Chiropractic Assistant license in November 2012. She moved to Washington County in 2004 with her husband, Nathan — a paramedic firefighter for Washington County — and their three children, and spent many years working and volunteering for Washington County Public Schools.
